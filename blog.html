Notes

The moments that changed how I work, written down before I forget what they felt like.

The day they gave me the department

Assistant manager at twenty-something, with a full course load and two side projects running. The lesson wasn’t about time management. It was that people will follow a plan they understand, and ignore a perfect one they don’t.

Catching the fridge before it failed

Monitoring refrigeration and HVAC on MT Alliance V8, I found anomalies in the alarm data and got a crew moving before product was lost. Real-time insight is worth nothing if nobody acts on it within the hour.

Pressure is a diagnostic

Systems tell you what they’re made of when they’re loaded, not when they’re idle. Same for people. I’ve stopped treating the hard weeks as something to survive and started treating them as the measurement.

More of these are coming.

Mostly about building things with AI in them and what breaks when real people use them.
