The day they gave me the department

Becoming an assistant manager with a full course load taught me something that wasn’t really about time management. People will follow a plan they understand, and ignore a perfect one they don’t.

Catching the cooler before it failed

Watching refrigeration and HVAC alarms, I noticed a pattern in the alarm data and got a crew moving before any product was lost. An alert only helps if someone acts on it within the hour. I kept that in mind when I built OpsRelay’s monitor.

Pressure is a diagnostic

Systems show what they’re made of under load, not when they’re idle, and people are the same. I’ve stopped treating hard weeks as something to get through and started treating them as the measurement.
