Privacy

What this site collects

Not much, and nothing that identifies you unless you choose to send a message.

Page analytics

If page analytics are turned on, they use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and doesn’t track you across other websites.

Visit notifications

Once per browser session, the site tells me that someone visited. I see the time, the first page you opened, the domain of the site that linked you here (if any), and whether you’re on a phone or a computer. It uses a random session ID stored only for this tab, and no cookies. Your IP address is used briefly to prevent abuse, kept only as a one-way keyed hash that expires within 26 hours, and never stored in its raw form or emailed. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, no notification is sent.

Contact form

Messages are delivered to my email by FormSubmit. I use your name and email only to reply, and I don’t add anyone to a list.
