About

Software, operations, and the space between them

I’m Taha. I’m in my fourth year of Computer Science at the University of Guelph, graduating in 2027. For the last few years I’ve been learning to build software and learning to run operations that can’t afford to stop, at the same time.

The operations side started as a part-time retail job at Loblaw. It became inventory control and SAP support, and now I’m an Assistant Department Manager leading 20+ associates. Every day I see what happens when a system’s numbers don’t match what’s on the shelf, and who ends up fixing it.

That shapes how I build. I want software that shows where its answers came from, fails in obvious ways, and still makes sense at 5am to the person who has to use it. It’s why I built OpsRelay’s monitoring around real user workflows, and why its repair agent has to prove a fix before anyone sees it.

I’m looking for 2027 roles in software engineering, applied AI, product, and corporate technology, especially on teams where engineering and operations work closely together.
