About me

I’m in my fourth year of Computer Science at the University of Guelph and graduate in 2027. For the past few years I’ve been learning to build software while working in operations that can’t afford to stop.

The operations side started with a part-time retail job at Loblaw. It became inventory control and SAP support, and now I’m an Assistant Department Manager leading more than 20 associates. I see every day what happens when a system’s numbers don’t match what’s on the shelf, and who ends up fixing it.

That shapes how I build. I want software that shows where its answers came from, fails in obvious ways, and still makes sense to the person using it at 5am. It’s why I built OpsRelay’s monitoring around real user workflows, and why its repair agent has to prove a fix before anyone sees it.

I’m interested in software engineering, applied AI, product and technology roles, especially on teams where engineering and operations work closely together.
