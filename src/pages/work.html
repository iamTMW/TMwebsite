OpsRelay

AI-assisted incident response, with a person in charge of every fix.

OpsRelay monitors two demo apps for real user-facing failures. When a probe fails, a Python agent reproduces the bug, diagnoses it from quoted evidence, and tests candidate fixes against the suite. A passing fix opens as a draft pull request, and a person decides whether to merge it.

My contributions

  • NovaMart: the demo storefront, with a Next.js front end, FastAPI checkout and pricing, and a side-effect-free checkout probe.
  • NovaConnect: the real-time messenger, using WebSockets, delivery and read receipts, and its own functional probe.
  • Monitoring: the first uptime monitor and status dashboard, including the incident lifecycle and the evidence hand-off to the agent.

Simply

A receipt manager for people who lose paper receipts and miss return windows. You photograph a receipt or type it in, review the details, and Simply organises purchases by store and category, shows monthly spending, and keeps return and warranty dates in view.

Capture goes through a parser interface. The current build uses a mock parser, and a model-backed parser is planned for the next milestone.

My contributions

  • The sign-in and sign-up screens, with form validation.
  • The shared receipt data model and mock data the team builds against.
  • The central theme and brand components.
