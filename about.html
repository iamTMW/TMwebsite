About me

Curious about technology. Grounded in the real world.

I'm a Computer Science student who enjoys understanding complex systems—and making them more useful to the people who rely on them.

Software, operations, and the space between.

I'm completing a Bachelor of Computing in Computer Science at the University of Guelph. Alongside my studies, I've worked in retail operations and progressed into a leadership role managing a department team.

That combination has influenced what I find interesting about technology. I like thinking about how software is designed, how data flows through a system, and how small improvements can help the people using it.

My interests span software engineering, business systems analysis, product development, and applied AI. I approach all of them with the same mindset: understand the problem before building the solution.
